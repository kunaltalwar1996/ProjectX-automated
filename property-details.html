About This Home

An architectural masterpiece situated in the most prestigious enclave of Bandra West. The Glass Pavilion offers unparalleled panoramic views of the city skyline and ocean beyond. Designed with an emphasis on seamless indoor-outdoor living, the property features retractable glass walls that open entirely to expansive terraces and a zero-edge infinity pool.

Interior finishes include rare imported stones, custom millwork, and state-of-the-art smart home integration. The primary suite is a sanctuary unto itself, boasting dual spa-like bathrooms and boutique-style dressing rooms. A truly exceptional offering for the discerning buyer seeking ultimate privacy and modern luxury.
